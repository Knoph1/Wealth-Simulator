IFB (Reinvested) IFB (Cash Payout) MMF (Net)
How CBK Infrastructure Bonds Work: IFBs pay coupons every 6 months (semi-annually) directly to your bank account with 0% Withholding Tax. Because CBK does not auto-reinvest coupons, manual reinvestment (via secondary market or sweep into high-yield instruments) unlocks the top green curve.
How Kenyan MMFs Work: MMFs accrue interest daily and compound monthly, automatically deducting 15% final Withholding Tax at month-end before crediting your unit balance. Top-ups are seamlessly invested on any business day.
